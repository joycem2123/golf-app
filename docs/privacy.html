Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

This policy explains what Clutch Up Golf ("Clutch Up", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored securely by our sign-in provider; we never see your password), first and last name, username, gender and Handicap Index.
  • Golf data: rounds, scores, games, events, teams, course corrections and courses you add, and optional stats you choose to track (putts, fairways, greens).
  • Friends: your friends list, friend requests, handicap update requests, and people you block or report.
  • Scorecard photos: only when you choose to scan a scorecard. The photo is sent to be read and is not kept after the course details are returned.
  • Notifications: a device token so we can send notifications you've allowed.
  • Guests can use Clutch Up without an email address; their data is tied to an anonymous account.

We don't collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run the app: keep score, share rounds and events with the friends you play with, show results, standings and records.
  • To send notifications you've allowed (for example, a friend asking you to update your handicap).
  • To keep the community safe (reports and blocks) and to provide support.

We don't sell your personal information and we don't show ads.

Who can see your information

  • Other players: your name, username and Handicap Index can be found by other users searching for friends. Rounds, events and courses are shared only with the people in them or the friends you choose.
  • Service providers who process data for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and server functions), Expo (app updates and notification delivery), Apple (push notifications), and Anthropic (reading scorecard photos and writing match summaries from scores; under their commercial terms, data sent through their API isn't used to train their models by default).
  • If required by law.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored with our service providers, which may be outside Canada (for example, in the United States), and protected by their security measures, including encryption in transit.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, friends, the rounds and events you started, shared courses and requests are deleted. Friends keep their own copies of rounds you played together. Reports you filed may be kept to protect the community.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your details any time in the app (Profile and Account information).
  • Delete your account in the app: More → Account information → Delete my account, or email us.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it by emailing us.

Children

Clutch Up isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
