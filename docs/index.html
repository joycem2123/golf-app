Support

Clutch Up Golf keeps score for golf matches between friends: match play, Nassau, skins, scrambles and more, live on everyone's phone, with trips, Ryder Cup–style cups, seasons and head-to-head records.

Contact us

Questions, problems or ideas? Email support@clutchupgolf.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

How do I start a round?

Tap New Round on Home, pick the course and tees, choose the players and teams, then the game. Friends on Clutch Up in the round see it live on their phones.

How do friends join my round?

Add them as friends (Friends tab) and include them in the round; it appears on their phone automatically. Anyone else can follow along with the round's join code.

My course isn't listed or the scorecard is wrong.

Go to More → Courses. You can correct ratings, slopes, pars and hole handicaps, add tees, or add a new course by hand or by scanning the scorecard.

How is my handicap used?

You enter your own Handicap Index in your Profile. Clutch Up uses it to work out strokes for each game. It isn't connected to Golf Canada or the USGA.

How do I report or block someone?

In the Friends tab, tap ⋯ next to the person and choose Report or Block. Blocked people can't find you, add you or share with you.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: More → Account information → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and its data. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.
