Built for your group

Every game. Every press.
Every bragging right.

Clutch Up keeps score so you can keep playing, and keeps the record of who's winning the rivalry.

⛳

The games you play

Singles and best-ball match play, Nassau with presses, skins, wolf, Vegas, Stableford, scramble, shamble and stroke play, with handicap strokes worked out for you.

⚡

Live for everyone

Scores update on every phone in the group as they're entered. Several matches at once, each with its own live status. Works without signal too.

🏆

Trips, cups & seasons

Plan a trip or a Ryder Cup–style cup with teams, rounds and matchups, or run a season on a schedule or as you go.

✍️

The story of every match

Results, hole-by-hole breakdowns and a written story of how it went: who came back, and where it turned.

⚔️

Head-to-head records

Every match you play builds your record against each friend. Finally, proof.

📷

Any course

Scan a scorecard and the course is ready to play, with every tee's rating, slope, pars and hole handicaps.

Live scoring

Tap, score, see who's up.

Team boxes show the ball that counts and who won the hole. The banner shows every match in your group, live.

  • Net or gross, off the low man or full handicaps
  • Several scorekeepers: the latest entry wins
  • Saves on your phone when there's no signal
Results

Celebrate the win. Relive the match.

Team scoreboards, colour-coded match results and every player's card. Tap a match for the hole-by-hole story, then share it with the group.

  • Out, In and Total for every player and team
  • A written story of every match
  • Share a results card to your group chat
Events

The trip. The cup. The season.

Set the teams once, plan the rounds and formats, and let the standings take care of themselves.

  • Ryder Cup–style team points
  • Season schedules, weekly or as you go
  • Player standings across every round
