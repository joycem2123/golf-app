## Description [4000]
Clutch Up Golf keeps score for the games you actually play with your friends, and keeps track of who's winning the rivalry.

PLAY YOUR GAMES
• Singles and best-ball match play, alternate shot, Nassau with presses, skins, wolf, Vegas, Stableford, scramble, shamble and stroke play
• Handicap strokes worked out for you: net or gross, full or off the low man, with the right allowance for each game
• Several matches in one group, each with its own live status

LIVE WITH YOUR GROUP
• Everyone in the round sees scores update live on their own phone
• Several scorekeepers? The latest entry wins, and everyone's told
• Works without signal: scores save on your phone and sync when you're back

TRIPS, CUPS AND SEASONS
• Plan a trip or a Ryder Cup–style cup: teams, rounds, formats and matchups
• Run a season on a schedule, or just play as you go
• Team and player standings update after every match

RESULTS AND BRAGGING RIGHTS
• Results with team scores, hole-by-hole match breakdowns and full scorecards
• A written story of every match: who came back, where it turned
• Head-to-head records and stats against everyone you play
• Share a results card with your group

YOUR COURSES
• Correct ratings, slopes, pars and hole handicaps for any course
• Add a course by scanning the scorecard, and share it with friends

FRIENDS
• Find friends by name or username and play together
• Keep everyone's handicap fresh with gentle reminders

Clutch Up Golf is for friendly games. Points have no cash value.

## Notes for the reviewer
Clutch Up Golf scores friendly golf games between friends. To try it: sign in with the demo account, open the live round on Home to see live scoring, or start a New Round (Willow Park) with the demo friends. Account deletion: More → Account information → Delete my account. Report/Block: Friends tab → ⋯ next to a friend. Game "points" have no monetary value; the app has no betting or payments. Scorecard scanning uses the camera only when the user taps "Scan the scorecard".
